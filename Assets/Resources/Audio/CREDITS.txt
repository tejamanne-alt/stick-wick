Sound credits

Most sound effects are from "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC
(https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as mirrored in
https://github.com/facebookincubator/meta-open-sound. The files were trimmed, layered, mixed
and loudness-matched for this game by tools/audio/build_audio.py.

Cinematic hits, booms, risers, reverse whooshes and some impacts and whooshes are from
SFX Kit (https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit), CC0 1.0 (public domain).

The CC BY 4.0 credit above must appear in the game's credits screen and store listing.

Story mode sounds are built by tools/audio/make_story_sfx.py: real recordings from the two
libraries above (breathing, whispers, the creepy drone and metal from the Oculus pack; thunder,
drips, the splash and the digital glitches from SFX Kit), layered and placed in a convolution
reverb; the tinnitus, heartbeat, wind, rotor, piano and cello are synthesised.
