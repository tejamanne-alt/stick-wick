# Stick Wick: the story, reworked (v2)

This replaces `story_arc.md` as the source of truth. The sets, fights and bosses we built stay. What changes:
- the reasons behind them;
- the lines;
- what the player does in the story moments;
- one short new chapter at the end.

---

## 1. What went wrong in v1

| Gap | Why it breaks the story |
|---|---|
| **Box 417 held nothing worth killing for** | Box 417 held a ring, passports and cash. Nobody hunts a man across a city for that, so the whole chase has no engine. |
| **Why is he alive?** | "Then finish him" says they meant to kill him, so why wait? The note only half answers it. |
| **He "remembers" the kidnapping** | He was unconscious in the warehouse when Maya was taken. He can't remember a scene he wasn't at. |
| **Voss appears from nowhere** | The villain is named, seen and explained only on the last roof. There's no build-up, so the reveal has nothing to land on. |
| **The three Voss stills all come at once** | The past is dumped in one speech at the end, instead of coming back piece by piece. |
| **Why take Maya and not kill her?** | No reason is given. |
| **The bosses are interchangeable** | The Driver, the Cleaner, the Handler and the Warden have no tie to him or to each other. |
| **The player only watches** | Between fights the player watches. They never choose, search, hold, or answer anything. |
| **The ending stops, it doesn't close** | Elena, the box, the life he chose: none of them are resolved. |

## 2. The truth (what really happened before chapter 1)

- **His past:** for fifteen years he was the best of VOSS's men. Voss took him in off the street at nineteen and taught him everything: to fight, to wait, to kill.
- **His insurance:** he kept a record of every job Voss ever gave him: names, dates, payments, in Voss's own hand on the orders. This is **THE BOOK**. It is enough to bury Voss, and him with it.
- **The family:** he met Elena, and Maya was born. Six years ago he told Voss: **"I'm out, Voss."**
- **The deal (the dead-man's switch):** he left the book in **box 417 at Meridian Trust**.
  - Only his living palm opens it: the scanner checks for a pulse.
  - The box carries a standing instruction: if he dies, his lawyer takes the box, and the book goes to the police.
  - So Voss can't kill him without burying himself. That deal kept them all alive for six years.
- **Voss's son:** VOSS has a real son, **SILAS**, raised in the hero's shadow ("He'd have done it cleaner"). The hero trained him. Silas is THE CLEANER.
- **The trigger (why now):** a detective, **HARROW** of Organised Crime, has been closing in on Voss. He has found out the hero exists and has twice come to the house. Elena wanted him to call Harrow back. Voss fears the hero will trade the book for immunity, so he strikes first.
- **Last night, the trap:** (the whole story takes one night, one day and one more night) Voss sent for him: one last meeting, the warehouse, "for old times." He went because Voss threatened Maya if he didn't.
  - In the warehouse a pipe came down from behind (**the blow**).
  - Voss needed him alive: only his living hand opens 417, and his death would send the book to the police.
  - The vault is time-locked until nine. The plan was to walk him in at opening with a gun in his back, take the book, then finish him.
- **The same night, at the house:**
  - Voss's order was careful: **"Bring them to me. All of them. Alive."** Elena and Maya were leverage, worth nothing dead.
  - **Silas disobeyed on purpose.** Elena ran for the back door with Maya, and he shot her. He wanted to take from the hero what the hero had chosen over his father.
  - Maya was dragged into the HANDLER's van.
  - Voss was furious: a body in the house, with Harrow already watching it. Silas's punishment: **"You made this mess. Go back and clean it. Leave nothing for Harrow."**
- **Today, at dusk, Silas goes back to the house** with plastic sheeting and bleach. Then the Driver radios in: "He's got the book. He's heading home." Silas stays, and waits for one evening. He wants to be the one who finishes the great man: "They always come home."
- **Then the blow did more than they meant.** He woke with no memory. The FOREMAN was told to keep him alive until the bank opened, walk him in, get the book, then finish him.

**So every chapter is one question answered, and one new question asked:**

| Ch | He learns | He now asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I'm a killer. They need me alive for box 417. | What's in the box? |
| 2 | I have a wife and a daughter. The box holds a book of my crimes, and someone wants it badly. | Where are they? |
| 3 | Elena is dead, killed by Silas, Voss's son, against Voss's order. Maya was taken. V raised me. | Where is Maya? |
| 4 | Maya is alive and talking to me. She's at Kessel Hill. The boss is Voss. | Can I get her back? |
| 5 | (the road) Who his daughter is: brave, funny, his. | Will I be the father she needs? |
| 6 | Everything. Voss did it to make me come back. | What am I now? |
| 7 | (the epilogue) He keeps his promise to Elena: he comes home. | |

## 5. Story controls (how the player takes part, not just watches)

One small set of gestures, the same all game. Each is shown once with a hint, then trusted.

| Gesture (mobile / keys) | Used for |
|---|---|
| **TAP an object** (glowing outline) / **E** | Look at a clue: the key, the photo, the drawing, the ribbon. |
| **HOLD** / **hold E** | Do something hard: push up off the floor, close Elena's eyes, press his palm to the scanner, hold the walkie's talk button. |
| **MASH** / **tap fast** | Break free: the zip ties in ch1, the Warden's choke. |
| **SWIPE in a direction** | Act fast in a cinematic: dodge the shot, grab a hand, throw. |
| **CHOOSE** (2 or 3 lines on screen) / **1 2 3** | Answer: what he says to the Cleaner, to Maya, to Voss. The choices change lines and small outcomes, not the ending's direction. |
| **DRAG** | Physical things: turn the photo over, pull the ribbon from the grass, hand over the book. |

Rules:
- No failure screens in story moments. If the player waits, the scene waits, or (in danger) it plays the safe outcome after a few seconds.
- No more than one interaction per beat, and never two beats in a row without a cut.

## 7. Shot design rules for every scene

- **A reason for every move:**
  - *Push in* = a realisation.
  - *Pull away* = loneliness or an ending.
  - *Low angle* = a threat or his resolve.
  - *High angle* = loss or smallness.
  - *Handheld* = danger.
  - *Locked off* = grief (camera_language.md).
- **One idea per shot:**
  - Each shot has one subject.
  - Shots of 2–4 seconds; a held 6-second shot only for grief (Elena, the grave).
- **Eyes first:** every reveal cuts to his face *before* the thing he sees, so the player reads the reaction.
- **Reverse shots for every conversation:**
  - Over-the-shoulder pairs.
  - Keep each speaker on the same side of the frame for the whole scene (no crossing the line).
- **Silence is a tool.** The music drops out at:
  - Elena;
  - the phone call;
  - the trade;
  - the grave.
- **The red is rationed:** the ribbon, the title line, blood and Voss's aim line. Nothing else is red.
