# Stick Wick: the story, level by level

**One look all game:** the fight-mode tower's black-and-white world: pale concrete, black steel, white edges, neutral light, wet reflective floors. Only the fighters carry colour.

**One subtitle rule all game:** `docs/story/dialogue.md`.

| # | Level | What happens | What he learns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Warehouse** | Wakes with a head wound. Escape, the knife, INSTINCT, the hall fight, the Foreman, the accidental shot. On the dead man: his own key, taken when they dumped him, and the boss's orders ("Get what's in 417 before the contract closes. Then finish him."). | **He is a hitman** ("You kill people. You are good at it.") |
| 2 | **The Bank** | Follows the key's logo to MERIDIAN TRUST. Box 417: a wedding ring, three passports, an envelope of cash with an address. The ring brings his wedding back. The clerk makes a call as he leaves. | **He has a wife: Elena.** And a home: 14 Alder Lane. |
| 2b | **The Street** (chapter 2's second half) | Straight out of the bank, the black car's men are waiting ("The box. Hand it over."). Four blocks of street fighting (the bank front, the market, under the railway with its trains, the crossroads) and THE DRIVER: a boxer, then a tire iron, then he drives his car at him. On the ground: "You'll find out at home. We got there first." (docs/story/chapter2_street.md) | That someone got to his home first. |
| 4 | **Home** | The family photo on his desk (him, Elena, their daughter): happy memories. Then he finds Elena's body, and remembers how she died. Rage. A fight inside the house. He ties the level boss to a chair and tortures him with electric shocks; the man never talks, and dies. | **His daughter.** Elena is dead. |
| 5 | **The Backyard** | Bloody footprints lead to the play area: slide, swings. Her favourite ribbon by the swing; he remembers the kidnapping ("PAPA!"). *"I will find you wherever you are in the world and bring you back. TRUST ME, my sweetheart!"* A fight in the play area. The mini-boss, broken, gives up the address. | **His daughter is alive**, taken to the fortress. |
| 6 | **The Hilltop Fortress** | The full gang, level by level, up the hill. | |
| 7 | **The Antagonist** | The drama, then the one-on-one, and the words that close the story. | The truth. |

**The phone call** (VOICE ON THE PHONE: "You were supposed to stay dead... the contract is open") is not in chapter 1 any more. It is kept for a later beat: the tortured boss's phone ringing in chapter 4 suits it best.
