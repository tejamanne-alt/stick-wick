## Chapter 1, new ending: the key, on the dead man (after the shot and "No... no... no...")

**Why it is realistic:** when the crew dumped him in the warehouse they searched him, as criminals would, and the Foreman kept what they found for his boss: the key, with his orders folded round it. So the key costs him something: he has to go through the pockets of the man he has just killed. And it explains everything that follows:
- why they kept him alive
- why the bank has a flag on the box
- why the clerk makes a call
- why chapter 2 is a race

## Chapter 2: The Bank

**Morning after the storm.** The street is still wet and the sky is white. No rain falls in this chapter or any later one: it stopped with the warehouse (puddles only, no rain sound). The bank is a stone facade with columns, a revolving door and a brass sign. Inside: a marble lobby with a clock, pendant lamps, teller windows behind glass, a velvet rope and a guard. A corridor runs behind it, through a barred gate, to the round vault door, which is open, and the safe-deposit room with its walls of numbered boxes.
