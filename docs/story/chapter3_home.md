# Chapter 3: HOME (design, for review)

14 Alder Lane, the same day, late afternoon going to dusk. It's dry: a low sun and long shadows through the windows, still in the game's black and white (the tower palette). The red is kept for the chapter's rage.

**What he learns:** he has a daughter, **Maya** (6). Elena is dead. Maya is alive and was taken. And the men who did it were *his* men once.

## The house (a cut-away, side-on, two storeys, like the warehouse)
- **Ground floor, left to right:** the porch and front door, the hall (stairs going up), the study, the living room (sofa, TV, a glass coffee table, the fireplace), and the kitchen (an island, a knife block, pans on hooks, a kettle), with the back door out to the garden. The garden and the play area are chapter 4.
- **Upstairs:** the landing, the bathroom, the master bedroom, and Maya's room (fairy lights, a toy chest, crayon drawings, a rocking horse).
- **Context kit:** slam a door on a man, throw a man through the glass coffee table, the kitchen island (face-plant), pans off the hooks (a weapon, three hits), the stairs (send a man down them), the banister, the wardrobe.

## Systems
- **RAGE** (this chapter only): the edges of the screen go red, strikes do 25% more, finishers come one hit sooner, and enemies hesitate more (the fear ring). There's no rampage: it's a mood, not a power-up.
- **Memories:** a new set of stills like Elena's (`render_memories.py`): a child figure, Maya (white, big eyes, a red ribbon). Her music-box theme becomes her motif for the rest of the game.
- **New sounds:** a music box, a creaking floorboard, a dripping tap, an electric buzz, a fridge hum, a swing's creak.
- **Test checkpoints:** the street (arrival), the study (photo and memory), the kitchen (Elena), the house fight (rooms 1–4), the Cleaner, the interrogation, and the footprints.

## Choices to confirm
1. The daughter's name: **Maya** (6).
2. How Elena died: shot at the back door after getting Maya out (the white flash only, nothing shown).
3. The Cleaner's line *"You taught me this"*: a first hint that the hero trained these men (the antagonist's world is his old one).
4. How far to take the interrogation: staged with restraint (light, sound, silhouette), not graphic.
