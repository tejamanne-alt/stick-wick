# Chapter 5: THE FORTRESS

Kessel Hill, the same night. **The storm breaks:** heavy rain, wind, lightning that really lights the place (a second bake of the whole set, lit by the strike, flickered in), forked bolts, thunder, rain running on the lens. The final chapters keep the storm.

**What happens:** he climbs to the fortress and fights through it, and at the end he sees them on the tower: Maya, and the man holding her. That man knows him.

## Coverage, not single shots
Every story beat is cut from several angles. The language:
- **Establishing:** wide and high, the place and the weather.
- **Medium:** him in the place.
- **Close and insert:** his face, his hands, the ribbon, a boot in a puddle.
- **Reverse:** what he sees.
- **High angle:** looking down on him, small; **low angle:** looking up, a threat or his resolve.
- **Handheld:** a little sway in the rain. **Slow motion** on the big hits. **Cuts timed to the lightning.**

## Systems
- **Storm:** rain that follows the camera (only outdoors), wind and rain sound (muffled inside), lightning every 6–12 s with thunder (sooner and louder as the night goes on), the set's lightning bake flickered in on each strike, forked bolts, rain on the lens, rippling wet ground.
- **Big-hit cinema:** a throw, a finisher or a knockout in this chapter sometimes gets a lightning strike, slow motion and a quick cut to a low angle.
- **Blackout:** after the generator goes, the courtyard is dark; the flashes are the light.
- **The Warden:** the story-boss kit (the chain = a pipe, the chandeliers = a timed hazard, bare hands).
- **Test checkpoints:** the road, the stair, the gate, the courtyard, the blackout, the Warden, the tower.
