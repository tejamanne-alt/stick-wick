# Every move, every chapter

**The combat isn't per chapter.** Every story chapter runs on the V4 body (`PrototypeBuilder`) and the story's combat (`HeroController.Contact` is true wherever the story runs), and none of the combat code checks which chapter it is. So every move and animation works in every chapter's fights:
- walking, running and the guard footwork (the stalk and the gliding back-step, bare and armed);
- jumps, landings and the armed leap and falling strike;
- the crouch, the sneak and the silent takedowns (bare and armed);
- the pick-ups (lying weapons, the lift into the hand, the pipe's heave, the katana's kneel, the scoops on the run, the bent-pipe swap);
- CROSSFIRE: bare, the pipe's four and the katana's four, with the pincer that sets it up in any fight;
- the weapon's block, the armed evasions (SLIPPED, NUKI-WAZA) and the armed hit reactions.

**What differs per chapter is what's in his hands.**

**Why the katana stays in chapter 6:** the story gives him the blade there. Voss's first lesson ("The line comes before the blade.") is in the fortress, and the katana moves are what that chapter is about. Putting a sword in the street or the house would spend that moment early.

**Where to try each:** the chapter 1 test checkpoints (the pipe, the katana, picking up, CROSSFIRE bare / pipe / katana); in the story, the pipes above, and the fortress for the katana.
